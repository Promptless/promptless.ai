Starport is a fully managed docs platform, built on the open-source Astro Starlight framework. Promptless stands up your documentation site, runs its builds and deploys, and keeps its content current as your product changes. The site runs on Promptless-managed hosting or on your own hosting account. Starport suits teams who want hosted docs-as-code that Promptless operates for them.

Two facts anchor everything below.

<Aside type="note">
  The documentation you are reading runs on Starport. Promptless migrated promptless.ai onto the Starport template and keeps it current.
</Aside>

Migration to Starport is included at no cost in every annual Promptless plan.

## What Starport gives you

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Managed hosting and builds" icon="rocket">
    Promptless runs your site's builds and deploys, so your team spends its time on content, not operations. Hosting runs on a Promptless-managed account or your own.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Agent-ready by default" icon="seti:default">
    Every Starport site ships llms.txt, a read-only docs MCP server, and page actions that make your docs consumable by AI agents.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Self-service configuration" icon="setting">
    Set site metadata, branding, search, redirects, and deploy options yourself after onboarding, in plain configuration files.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Kept current by the agent" icon="approve-check">
    The Promptless agent detects when your product changes and opens documentation pull requests against the Git repository you own.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
