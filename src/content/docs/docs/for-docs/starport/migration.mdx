Moving your docs onto Starport is a fully managed switch. Promptless does the work: it stands up the site, moves your content and configuration, and cuts you over. You review the result and, after onboarding, maintain the site yourself. The sections below cover who does what, what you keep, and what happens once you are live. For the fundamentals of docs-as-code and platform choice, see [Understand docs-as-code](/docs/for-docs/migrate/why-docs-as-code) and [Choose a platform](/docs/for-docs/migrate/choose-a-platform).

## What Promptless handles

Promptless runs the switch end to end, so your team keeps writing while the migration happens around you.

<Steps>

1. Promptless bootstraps your site on the Starport template.

2. Promptless migrates your content, components, screenshots, and configuration onto it.

3. Promptless optimizes your docs for agent accessibility, so the agent-ready surfaces work from day one.

4. Promptless cuts your traffic over to the new site.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
  The switch is low-risk: your content moves as source files, you review the result before cutover, and Promptless keeps the migrated site feature-complete.
</Aside>

## What Promptless needs from you

The managed switch needs a few things from you:

- Access to your existing documentation content.
- Your Git repository, or Promptless sets one up with you.
- Control of your domain's DNS, so the site serves at your custom domain at cutover.

## What you keep

Your content stays yours. It lives in a Git repository your team owns, as Markdown and MDX source files you can edit at any time. You keep the full history, the access control, and the ability to publish directly. Promptless operates the site around that repository.

Because your content lives in your own Git repository on the open-source Astro Starlight framework, you keep it if you stop using Promptless. The site keeps building and serving on its own, and nothing about it depends on Promptless-proprietary technology.

## URLs and redirects

When URLs change during the move, Promptless sets up redirects so existing links keep working. For the fundamentals, see [Preserve URLs and redirects](/docs/for-docs/migrate/preserve-urls-and-redirects).

## After onboarding

Once you are live, the site is yours to run. You tune it yourself through its configuration files; see [Configure your Starport site](/docs/for-docs/starport/configuring-starport) for the options you own. The Promptless agent keeps your content current as your product changes; see [Keep your Starport docs current](/docs/for-docs/starport/keep-docs-current) for how that works.

## Cost

Every annual Promptless plan includes migration to Starport at no cost. Your content moves onto the hosted stack as part of your plan.

<Aside type="note">
  To start a migration, talk to your Promptless contact, or the Promptless team if you are evaluating.
</Aside>
