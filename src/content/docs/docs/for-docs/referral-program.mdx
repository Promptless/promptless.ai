## Overview

Know another docs team that's struggling with docs debt? Introduce them to Promptless by October 31, 2026. When they become a paying customer, you get $2,000 or a trip to a docs conference, paid for by Promptless.

For every referral that becomes a paying customer, pick one reward:

| Reward          | What you get                                                                                                                                                |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cash            | $2,000.                                                                                                                                                     |
| Conference trip | Promptless sends you or a teammate to the docs conference closest to you, with the ticket, travel, and hotel covered. The trip is worth about $2,000–4,000. |

Pick the conference from this list:

- [Write the Docs Australia](https://www.writethedocs.org/conf/) in Melbourne, December 3–4, 2026.
- [CIDM ConVEx](https://convex.infomanagementcenter.com/) in Seattle, April 26–28, 2027.
- [Write the Docs Portland](https://www.writethedocs.org/conf/portland/2027/), May 2–4, 2027.
- Write the Docs Berlin, for customers in Europe. The 2027 dates aren't announced yet.

You can refer as many docs teams as you like, and each one that becomes a customer earns its own reward. For example, 10 new customers earn you $20,000, or conference trips for your whole team.

## Before you start

Anyone who works at a Promptless customer can refer, including paying customers, pilot customers, and open source projects that use Promptless.

A referral counts when all of these are true:

- You make the introduction by October 31, 2026. The team you refer can take as long as they need to sign and pay after that date.
- The company is new to Promptless. It isn't a current customer, and it hasn't talked with Promptless in the last six months.
- You're the first person to introduce that company. If two people introduce the same company, the first introduction counts.

## Refer a docs team

- By October 31, 2026, introduce the team to Manny or Prithvi at Promptless, by email or in your shared Slack channel with Promptless.

Promptless replies to every introduction within one business day to set up a first call. When the company you referred makes its first payment, Promptless sends your reward.
