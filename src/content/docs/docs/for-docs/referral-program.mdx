Know another docs team that's drowning in docs debt? Introduce them to Promptless! When they become a paying customer, you get $2,000 or a trip to a docs conference on us.

## Who can refer

Anyone who works at a Promptless customer can refer. That includes paying customers, pilot customers, and open source projects that use Promptless.

## What you get

For every referral that becomes a paying customer, pick the reward you want:

- **Cash:** $2,000.
- **Conference trip:** We send you or a teammate to the docs conference closest to you, with the ticket, travel, and hotel covered. That's a trip worth about $2,000–4,000. Pick from:
  - [Write the Docs Australia](https://www.writethedocs.org/conf/) in Melbourne, December 3–4, 2026.
  - [CIDM ConVEx](https://convex.infomanagementcenter.com/) in Seattle, April 26–28, 2027.
  - [Write the Docs Portland](https://www.writethedocs.org/conf/portland/2027/), May 2–4, 2027.
  - Write the Docs Berlin, for customers in Europe. The 2027 dates aren't announced yet.

You can refer as many docs teams as you'd like! If you introduce us to 10 new customers, you could earn $20,000 or send your entire team to a docs conference on us.

## How to refer someone

Introducing a team takes one email or Slack message. By October 31, 2026, introduce the team to Manny or Prithvi at Promptless by email or in your shared Slack channel with Promptless.

We reply to every introduction within one business day to set up a first call, so your contact hears from us fast.

## Which referrals qualify

A referral counts when:

- You make the introduction by October 31, 2026. The team you refer can take as long as they need to sign and pay after that date.
- The company is new to Promptless. It isn't a current customer, and it hasn't talked with Promptless in the last six months.
- You're the first person to introduce that company. If two people introduce the same company, the first introduction counts, so don't wait!

## When you get paid

We send your reward as soon as the company you referred makes its first payment. An unpaid pilot doesn't count as a first payment.
