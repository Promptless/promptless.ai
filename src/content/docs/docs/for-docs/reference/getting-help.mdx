Contact the Promptless team when something doesn't look right, or when you want help getting more out of Promptless. The team can help with:

- **Events Promptless processed**: A trigger, suggestion, or other event that doesn't look right. It can come from a code PR, a Slack message, or another source, and the team investigates it.
- **Your setup**: Integrations, triggers, doc collections, and other configuration. The team can walk through it with you.
- **How the agent behaves**: Guiding Promptless's behavior, adjusting its preferences, or customizing how it handles your docs.
- **Anything else**: Any other question about Promptless.

## Contact us

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Email support">
    For questions, feedback, or troubleshooting, reach us at [help@gopromptless.ai](mailto\:help@gopromptless.ai).
  </Card>

  <Card title="Slack Connect">
    During onboarding, the team sets up a shared Slack channel between your team and Promptless for direct support.
  </Card>

  <Card title="From your editor">
    If you've connected Promptless over MCP, ask your editor to report the problem. It sends your report to our team with the [`message_promptless_support`](/docs/for-docs/connect/triggers/mcp#message_promptless_support) tool.
  </Card>

  <LinkCard title="Schedule a call" href="https://cal.com/team/promptless/30m-all" description="Book a 30-minute call with the Promptless team to talk through your questions or get hands-on help." />
</CardGrid>
