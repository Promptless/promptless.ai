**Used for: Triggers**

<Aside type="note">
  The Discord integration is in beta. Contact [help@gopromptless.ai](mailto\:help@gopromptless.ai) for more information about enabling this feature for your organization. Until Discord is enabled for your organization, the Discord card on the Integrations page may read **Contact Promptless** instead of a connect button.
</Aside>

Promptless integrates with Discord through a single Promptless-owned Discord app, enabling documentation updates from your server's conversations.

Promptless does not archive or store your Discord messages.

Disclaimer: Promptless uses LLMs from OpenAI and Anthropic that have the potential to generate inaccurate results.

## Prerequisites

To connect Discord to Promptless, you'll need:

- An organization administrator on the Promptless [Integrations page](https://app.gopromptless.ai/integrations)
- The ability to add a bot to the Discord server you want to connect

## Connect Discord

1. On the [integrations page](https://app.gopromptless.ai/integrations), click **Add to Discord** on the Discord card.
2. On Discord's add-bot screen, choose the server you want to connect and approve the requested permissions.
3. Discord returns you to Promptless, which automatically identifies the server and finishes the connection.
4. Verify that Discord shows as connected on the integrations page.

To remove access, click **Disconnect Discord** on the connected Discord card. **Check access** re-pulls the server and its current channel list, so you can confirm what Promptless can currently reach and refresh the channel list after adding channels.

## What you can do with Discord

Once connected, you can use Discord for:

- **[Triggers](/docs/for-docs/connect/triggers/discord-messages)**: Tag @Promptless in a channel or enable passive listening to trigger documentation updates
- **[Configuration](/docs/for-docs/get-the-most-out/ask-promptless-to-update-config)**: Ask Promptless to update your triggers, doc collections, and other configuration directly

## Key permissions

When you add the bot, Discord asks you to approve these permissions:

- **View Channels** — See the channels you point Promptless at
- **Read Message History** — Read the conversation context in those channels when triggered
- **Send Messages** — Post suggestion updates and replies
- **Embed Links** — Render suggestion links in its messages
- **Send Messages in Threads** — Reply within threads

<Aside type="note" title="Understanding Discord permissions">
  By default, Promptless only accesses Discord content when you @mention it. Passive listening is opt-in and limited to the channels you select.
</Aside>

## Privacy and channel access

Promptless reads Discord content only when you @mention it or in channels you explicitly add to passive listening.

For more details about working with suggestions in chat, see [Review from Slack and Teams](/docs/for-docs/work-the-queue/reviewing-from-slack-and-teams).
