Single sign-on (SSO) lets your team sign in to Promptless through your organization's existing identity provider (IdP).

## Overview

Set up SSO when your organization manages access to its tools through an IdP, or when a security review requires it before rollout. The Promptless team configures SSO for you. You collect configuration details from your IdP, send them to Promptless, and then add the service provider details Promptless returns to your IdP.

With SSO, your IdP controls access to Promptless:

- Control Promptless access through your existing identity management workflows.
- Enforce your IdP's multi-factor authentication (MFA) requirements.
- Apply consistent session handling across your organization's applications. See [Session management](/docs/for-docs/security/compliance-and-certifications#session-management).
- Keep authentication and access logs for compliance requirements.

### Supported authentication methods

Promptless supports these SSO methods:

| Method                | Supported identity providers                                                                                |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| SAML 2.0              | Enterprise identity providers, including Active Directory Federation Services, Okta, OneLogin, and Azure AD |
| OpenID Connect (OIDC) | Google Workspace, Azure AD, and other OIDC-compliant providers                                              |
| Direct integrations   | Google Workspace and GitHub Enterprise authentication                                                       |

## Before you start

<Aside type="note">
  SSO is available only for enterprise customers. To learn about enterprise plans and pricing, email [help@gopromptless.ai](mailto\:help@gopromptless.ai).
</Aside>

To request SSO, you need:

- An active Promptless enterprise subscription
- Administrative access to your identity provider
- The ability to configure SAML assertions or OIDC claims for user attributes
- A verified domain in your Promptless organization settings. See [Domain verification and auto-enrollment](/docs/for-docs/reference/account-management#domain-verification-and-auto-enrollment).

## Request SSO configuration

Your IT team and the Promptless team set up SSO together:

<section id="requesting-sso-configuration-steps" class="pl-site-steps is-compact" data-sticky-heading="false">
  <div class="pl-site-step-list">
    <article class="pl-site-step" id="collect-identity-provider-information">
      <span class="pl-site-step-num">1</span>
      <h3>Collect identity provider information</h3>
      <p>From your identity provider, gather the details for your authentication method.</p>
      <p>For SAML 2.0:</p>

      <ul>
        <li>Identity provider entity ID or issuer URL</li>
        <li>Single sign-on service URL</li>
        <li>X.509 certificate, the public key for signature verification</li>
        <li>Attribute mappings for the email, first name, and last name fields</li>
      </ul>

      <p>For OpenID Connect (OIDC):</p>

      <ul>
        <li>Issuer URL, the discovery endpoint</li>
        <li>Client ID and client secret</li>
        <li>Authorization endpoint and token endpoint URLs</li>
        <li>Supported scopes and user attribute claims</li>
      </ul>
    </article>

    <article class="pl-site-step" id="submit-configuration-request">
      <span class="pl-site-step-num">2</span>
      <h3>Submit the configuration request</h3>
      <p>Send these details to your dedicated account manager, or <a href="mailto:help@gopromptless.ai">email support</a>:</p>

      <ul>
        <li>Your organization name and verified domain</li>
        <li>Your preferred authentication method, SAML 2.0 or OIDC</li>
        <li>The identity provider information from step 1</li>
      </ul>
    </article>

    <article class="pl-site-step" id="configuration-and-validation">
      <span class="pl-site-step-num">3</span>
      <h3>Complete the configuration with the Promptless team</h3>
      <p>The Promptless team configures the SSO integration and sends you:</p>

      <ul>
        <li>Service provider (SP) metadata to add to your identity provider's configuration</li>
        <li>The user attribute mapping and role assignment configuration</li>
      </ul>
    </article>
  </div>
</section>

After SSO is active, your team signs in through your IdP:

- People who open Promptless are redirected to your organization's sign-in portal.
- After they authenticate with your IdP, they have immediate access to Promptless.
- Promptless creates accounts for new users from their IdP profile, through just-in-time provisioning.
- User access levels can sync from your identity management system.

For questions about SSO setup or your organization's authentication requirements, email the enterprise support team at [help@gopromptless.ai](mailto\:help@gopromptless.ai). The Promptless team works directly with your IT department on the setup.
