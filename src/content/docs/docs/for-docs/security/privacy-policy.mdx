Promptless practices data minimization. It accesses only what it needs, when it needs it, and stores only what it needs to run the service. This page summarizes the privacy principles behind that approach, the options for organizations with stricter requirements, and where to find the full legal privacy policy.

## Use cases

- Answer a privacy reviewer's questions about data retention, model training, and integration scope with a short, forwardable summary.
- Decide whether your organization needs self-hosting or a customer-managed model deployment to meet its privacy requirements.
- Find the full legal privacy policy, or exercise your data rights.

## Privacy principles

Promptless handles your data according to these principles:

| Principle                                                  | What it means                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Promptless doesn't store your source code or conversations | When Promptless analyzes a GitHub pull request or Slack thread, it processes that content in real time to generate documentation suggestions, then discards it. Promptless keeps no copies of your source code, support conversations, Jira tickets, or other integration data. |
| Promptless doesn't train models on your data               | Promptless doesn't use your content to pre-train or fine-tune language models. It uses your code, documentation, and conversations only to generate your documentation suggestions.                                                                                             |
| Promptless stores only what's necessary                    | Promptless stores copies of your documentation to track changes and generate updates. It also stores the feedback you provide to improve suggestion quality, and the credentials it uses to connect to your tools.                                                              |
| Your data is encrypted                                     | Data in transit is encrypted with TLS 1.2 or higher. Data at rest is encrypted with AES-256.                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| You control the integration scope                          | Promptless accesses only the repositories, channels, or projects you configure, and requests no broad organizational access.                                                                                                                                                    |

For the full classification of what Promptless stores and processes, see [Data handling and classification](/docs/for-docs/security/data-handling-and-classification).

## Enterprise privacy options

Organizations with stricter requirements have two options:

| Option                                               | What it keeps in your control                                                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Self-hosting](/docs/for-docs/security/self-hosting) | Promptless runs on your own infrastructure, and no data leaves your environment. |
| Customer-managed model deployments                   | External AI providers never process your content.                                |

## Full privacy policy and data rights

The [Promptless Privacy Policy](https://promptless.ai/privacy) is the full legal policy. It covers data collection, retention, cookies, and your rights.

For questions about privacy practices, or to exercise your data rights, email [help@gopromptless.ai](mailto\:help@gopromptless.ai).
