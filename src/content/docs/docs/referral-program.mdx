If you use Promptless and know another docs team that could use it, introduce us. When that team becomes a paying customer, Promptless pays you $2,000 or sends you or a teammate to a docs conference.

## Who can refer

Any employee of a Promptless customer can refer, including paying customers, pilot customers, and open source projects that use Promptless.

## What you get

For each referral that becomes a paying customer, choose one of these rewards:

- **Cash:** $2,000.
- **Conference trip:** Promptless sponsors you or a teammate to attend the docs conference closest to you. The trip covers the ticket, travel, and hotel, worth about $2,000–4,000. Options include:
  - [Write the Docs Australia](https://www.writethedocs.org/conf/) in Melbourne, December 3–4, 2026.
  - [CIDM ConVEx](https://convex.infomanagementcenter.com/) in Seattle, April 26–28, 2027.
  - [Write the Docs Portland](https://www.writethedocs.org/conf/portland/2027/), May 2–4, 2027.
  - Write the Docs Berlin, for customers in Europe. The 2027 dates aren't announced yet.

There's no limit on the number of referrals per person. Ten referrals that become customers earn $20,000.

## How to refer someone

By October 31, 2026, introduce the team to Manny or Prithvi at Promptless. Send the introduction by email or post it in your shared Slack channel with Promptless.

Promptless replies to each introduction within one business day to book a first call.

## Which referrals qualify

A referral qualifies when:

- You make the introduction by October 31, 2026. The referred company can take as long as it needs to sign and pay after that date.
- The company is new to Promptless. It isn't a current customer, and it hasn't been in talks with Promptless in the last six months.
- You're the first person to introduce that company. If two people introduce the same company, the first introduction counts.

## When you get paid

Promptless pays the reward after the referred company makes its first payment. An unpaid pilot doesn't count as a first payment.
