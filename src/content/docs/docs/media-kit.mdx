## Usage guidelines

Follow these guidelines when you use Promptless brand assets:

| Guideline                             | Details                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Use the assets unmodified             | Use the official logos and colors on this page as they are. Don't stretch, recolor, rotate, add effects to, or otherwise alter the logo. |
| Keep the logo legible                 | Give the logo clear space, and choose the light or dark variant to suit the background.                                                  |
| Represent the relationship accurately | Don't imply a partnership or endorsement that doesn't exist.                                                                             |
