The suggestions list now works as a proper review queue. Each suggestion is a row. Rows group by priority by default. Expand any row to see the description, changed files, and details, and edit assignees, priority, and labels in place without opening the suggestion.

If your team is processing more than a handful of suggestions at a time, the new layout makes it faster to triage what is urgent, assign it to the right person, and move on.

## The problem

The old list put all suggestions in a flat feed. To see what a suggestion was about, you had to open it. To assign it or change its priority, you had to open it. If you were working through a backlog, that meant a lot of clicking in and out.

Teams running multiple doc collections had no way to see at a glance which suggestions came from which collection, or which ones were still unassigned. Filtering by a label or an assignee meant typing the exact value, with no hints from the system.

## What changed

Each suggestion is a single row showing its title, trigger source, and a status pill. Rows group by priority by default. You can switch the grouping to doc collection or created date.

Select a row to expand it inline. The expanded row shows the suggestion's description, the changed files, and its details panel. From there, edit the assignees, priority, and labels directly. No modal, no navigation. Close the row and the list stays where you left it.

The search field now shows each active filter as a removable pill. Start typing a qualifier and it autocompletes from your organization's labels, assignees, doc collections, and trigger sources.

## Who benefits most

Teams with more than one person reviewing documentation suggestions will notice the biggest difference. The grouped view gives a clear picture of what needs attention during a triage session. Inline editing means you can assign and label a batch of suggestions without opening each one.

Teams with multiple doc collections will also find the collection grouping useful for working through suggestions by product area.

## How to use it

No setup required. The new layout is the default for all organizations. Open the **Suggestions** tab to see it. Use the **Group by** control to switch between priority, collection, and date.

Filters you apply are saved and restored on your next visit. Shared and bookmarked filter URLs continue to work.

See [Suggestions list](/docs/for-docs/work-the-queue/web-interface#suggestions-list) in the docs for details.
