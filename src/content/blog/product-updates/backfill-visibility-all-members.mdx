Backfill status and the documentation suggestions a backfill produces are now visible to all organization members. Before this change, only admins could see them.

Previewing and launching a backfill still require admin access. But reviewing the output no longer does.

## The problem

A backfill is typically run by an admin to generate documentation suggestions for a large batch of code, catching up on months or years of changes at once. The suggestions it produces are most useful when reviewed by the people who actually know the relevant code. In most organizations, those people aren't admins.

Before this change, non-admin members had no way to see what a backfill produced. An admin had to relay the information manually, or grant access they might not want to give broadly. The result: backfill output sat in a restricted corner while the best reviewers were locked out.

## What changed

Backfill status, progress indicators, and all suggestions produced by a backfill are now visible to every organization member. The view is read-only for non-admins. They can't preview or launch a backfill, but they can follow the run in progress and review what it produced.

This is the same output admins see. No special view, no filtered subset.

## Who benefits most

Teams where documentation review is shared across contributors who don't have admin access. If your org runs backfills to catch up legacy documentation, the engineers or technical writers best positioned to evaluate those suggestions can now do so directly, without a handoff.

It's also useful for teams that want visibility without access. A manager or lead who wants to track backfill progress can now do so without needing to be made an admin.

## How to use it

No configuration required. Organization members can find backfill status and suggestions in the Promptless dashboard where admins see them. Previewing and launching a backfill remain admin-only. See [Backfill suggestions](/docs/for-docs/connect/triggers#backfill-suggestions) for details on what's available.

If you've been routing backfill output through an admin intermediary, that step is gone.
