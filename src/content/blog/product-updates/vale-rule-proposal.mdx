When Promptless analyzes your doc collection, it now proposes Vale rules derived from your style guidance. If it can place them in your repository, it opens a suggestion to bring them in.

## The problem

Vale is a prose linter. It catches style violations automatically in CI. But writing Vale rules requires knowing Vale's rule format and deciding which parts of your style guidance are specific enough to enforce mechanically. That's a separate skill from writing the style guidance itself.

Teams with detailed style guidance often end up without Vale rules that match it. They document what good writing looks like, but nothing enforces it in CI. Violations accumulate.

The gap between "we wrote down the rule" and "the linter catches violations" usually stays open because closing it takes time no one sets aside.

## What it does now

During doc collection analysis, Promptless reads your style guidance and identifies rules that can be expressed in Vale's format. It proposes those rules, records them in your Agent Knowledge Base under a `style/vale/` area, and opens a suggestion to place them in your documentation repository.

You review the proposed rules in the suggestion before anything merges. Guidance your style guide states becomes an enforced rule at error or warning severity. A strong convention Promptless observed across your docs becomes an advisory rule at suggestion severity. You decide whether an advisory rule gets promoted to an enforced one. Promptless never promotes a rule on its own.

This works alongside any Vale setup you already have. Promptless isn't replacing your existing rules. It's filling in coverage for the parts of your style guidance that weren't yet enforced.

## Who benefits most

Teams that have written down their voice and style preferences but haven't translated them into Vale rules. If your style guide exists and Vale is already part of your docs pipeline, this shortens the path to enforcement.

If your team doesn't use Vale yet, Promptless bootstraps a Vale configuration and includes it in the same suggestion, so the rules have somewhere to run. Nothing lands in your repository until you review and merge it.

## How to use it

No configuration is needed. Promptless proposes Vale rules as part of doc collection analysis, which runs when you connect a docs repository. When it can place the rules in your repository, the proposal appears as a suggestion in your dashboard. The rules are also recorded in your Agent Knowledge Base under `style/vale/`.

See [Propose Vale rules from your style guidance](/docs/for-docs/audit/standards-enforcement#propose-vale-rules-from-your-style-guidance) for details.
