**Improvements:**

* **Keep the Open in Promptless link while following your PR template:** Add `[Open in Promptless]({suggestion_url})` to your PR or merge request template where the link belongs. Promptless fills in your template as usual and replaces `{suggestion_url}` with the suggestion's dashboard URL. See [Use your own PR template](/docs/for-docs/connect/doc-locations/use-your-own-pr-template).
