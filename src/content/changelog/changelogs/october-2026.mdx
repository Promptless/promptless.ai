**New Features:**

* **Audit your docs for broken links:** You can now ask Promptless to audit your published docs site. It reports broken links, missing anchors, and links that permanently redirect to a new URL. Ask from Slack, Microsoft Teams, or the dashboard, and Promptless fixes the links in your docs source and opens a suggestion. Put the audit on a schedule trigger to run it every week. See [Example: Audit your docs for broken links](/docs/for-docs/get-the-most-out/teach-promptless-a-custom-task/audit-broken-links).
