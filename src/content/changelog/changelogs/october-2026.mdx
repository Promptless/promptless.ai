**New Features:**

* **Report a problem to Promptless from your editor:** Your MCP client can now report a problem to the Promptless team with the `message_promptless_support` tool. The report includes your organization, your name and email, and your editor's client name. You describe what went wrong and how urgent it is. It's available on both MCP server URLs to every signed-in organization member. See [`message_promptless_support`](/docs/for-docs/connect/triggers/mcp#message_promptless_support).
