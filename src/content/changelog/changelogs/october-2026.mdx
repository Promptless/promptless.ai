**Improvements:**

* **Redirects for changed page URLs:** A suggestion that retires, moves, or renames a page now adds a permanent redirect from the old URL. The redirect leads to the page that now covers the topic and sits next to your collection's existing redirects. The same suggestion points links elsewhere in the collection at the new page. For a feature that's deprecated but still works, Promptless keeps the page and adds a deprecation notice that links to the replacement. See [Redirects and Promptless](/docs/for-docs/migrate/preserve-urls-and-redirects#redirects-and-promptless).
