**New Features:**

* **Free trial with no card required:** Finishing the setup wizard starts a free trial with no card required. The trial lasts 14 days, or 90 days on the Promptless YC deal. During the trial, admins add a card from **Settings** → **Billing**. The page shows the recommended plan and the trial's end date. See [Add a card](/docs/for-docs/reference/account-management#add-a-card).

**Improvements:**

* **Free trial status and reminders:** During a free trial, a dashboard banner shows the days remaining and an **Add a card** button. Promptless emails the billing contact three days before the trial ends and again when it ends. After the trial ends, Promptless finishes running tasks and starts no new documentation work until an admin adds a card. API and MCP requests return a `trial_expired` error (`402` over the API). See [Free trial](/docs/for-docs/reference/account-management#free-trial).
