**Improvements:**

* **Review changes in place on the suggestion page:** On the **Review Suggestion** tab, comments and citations sit under the lines they refer to. Collapse them to one line when you're done reading. Each separate change in a modified file has its own **Undo**. Use it to restore the original text of one change and keep the rest of the suggestion. To start a comment, hover over a line number and select **+**. The actions bar tracks how many files you've marked **Viewed** and has a **Hide citations** toggle. See [Review the changed files](/docs/for-docs/work-the-queue/web-interface#review-the-changed-files).
