**New Features:**

* **Message Promptless support from your editor:** The Promptless MCP server has a new tool, `message_promptless_support`, that sends a message to the Promptless team. Your editor can use it to flag problems as they come up, so our team can get on them. You can also use it to report bugs or request features. See [`message_promptless_support`](/docs/for-docs/connect/triggers/mcp#message_promptless_support).
