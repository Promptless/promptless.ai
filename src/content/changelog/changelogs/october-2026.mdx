**Improvements:**

* **Weekly backlog chart on the Impact page:** The Impact page (**Settings** → **Impact**) charts the last 4, 8, or 12 weeks as a waterfall. Each week steps up by suggestions created, down by those merged and closed, and lands on the backlog still open. Hover a week for its counts and its open backlog by priority, or select priorities and turn on **Only these** to chart just those. See [Measure the impact of Promptless](/docs/for-docs/measure/reporting-and-roi#what-you-can-observe-today).
