**Improvements:**

* **Jira tickets carry only substantive replies:** Promptless comments on a Jira issue when it replies with the results of its work. The automatic eyes emoji (👀) receipt comment from September is retired. This applies to the built-in label, mention, and assignment triggers and to your configured triggers. See [Reply on the ticket](/docs/for-docs/connect/triggers/jira#reply-on-the-ticket).
