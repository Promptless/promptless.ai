**Improvements:**

* **Keep the Open in Promptless link while following your PR template:** If your repository has a PR or merge request template, add `[Open in Promptless]({suggestion_url})` where you want the link, such as below your checklist. Promptless fills in your template as usual and replaces `{suggestion_url}` with the suggestion's dashboard URL. This also works with `publishing.suppress_pr_description_extras` on, so you can drop the other generated extras and still keep the link. See [Place the suggestion link yourself](/docs/for-docs/connect/doc-locations/github-repos#place-the-suggestion-link-yourself).
