**Deprecated:**

- **Slack follow-ups for resolved suggestions retired:** Promptless has retired its Slack thread replies for merged, closed, and archived suggestions. The **Suggestion Status Updates** organization setting is retired with them. Your notification channels still get announcements for new and updated suggestions. See [Customize notifications](/docs/for-docs/tune/notifications).
