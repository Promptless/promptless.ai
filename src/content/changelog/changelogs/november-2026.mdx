**Improvements:**

- **Screenshots appear on each suggestion:** A suggestion shows its screenshots as thumbnails on its row in the suggestions list. They also appear in a **Screenshots** section at the top of the suggestion. A screenshot the suggestion commits to your docs repository is tagged **New**, **Updated**, or **Removed**. Select a thumbnail to open that screenshot on the suggestion's **Review Suggestion** tab. These thumbnails replace the Screenshot Updates tab in the dashboard sidebar. See [Review screenshot changes](/docs/for-docs/get-the-most-out/screenshots#review-screenshot-changes).
