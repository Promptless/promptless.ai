**Improvements:**

- **One set of style rules for your organization:** Every connected doc collection uses one set of style rules, kept under `style/rules/` in your Agent Knowledge Base. Give Promptless a style guide as a file, link, Google Drive document, or message, and it saves a copy as a source for the rules. Stated rules no Vale check can express become judgment rules that Promptless applies while drafting. To keep the rules in a repository of your own, name it in `style.rules_collection`. When you connect a collection and your organization already has rules, Promptless suggests fixes for the collection's existing violations of your stated rules. Rules Promptless already wrote under `style/vale/` move to `style/rules/`. When your Knowledge Base held rules for more than one collection, the next analysis builds the one set from all your sources. See [Turn your style guidance into rules](/docs/for-docs/audit/standards-enforcement#turn-your-style-guidance-into-rules).
- **Screenshots on every suggestion:** Screenshot thumbnails now appear on each suggestion's row and at the top of the suggestion, replacing the Screenshot Updates tab. Catch a wrong or stale screenshot while you triage, and filter with `has:screenshots` to review every visual change in one pass. See [Review screenshot changes](/docs/for-docs/get-the-most-out/screenshots#review-screenshot-changes).
- **Voice messages in Slack:** Promptless transcribes voice messages and audio files in threads you trigger it from and uses what was said in the suggestion. Talk through a change when that's faster than writing it up. See [Include images and files](/docs/for-docs/connect/triggers/slack-messages#include-images-and-files).
