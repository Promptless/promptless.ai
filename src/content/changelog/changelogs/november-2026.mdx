**New Features:**

- **Review and edit style rules in the dashboard:** The dashboard's **Rules** page, under **Style guide**, lists your organization's Vale rules. Each rule shows how many violations it has on your connected doc collections. Open a rule to see the style guide passage or docs convention it came from, and change its level, message, or patterns. Admins save edits to your Agent Knowledge Base, and later analyses keep a rule you edited as you wrote it. See [Review and edit style rules in the dashboard](/docs/for-docs/audit/style-rules-in-the-dashboard).

**Improvements:**

- **Screenshots on every suggestion:** Screenshot thumbnails now appear on each suggestion's row and at the top of the suggestion, replacing the Screenshot Updates tab. Catch a wrong or stale screenshot while you triage, and filter with `has:screenshots` to review every visual change in one pass. See [Review screenshot changes](/docs/for-docs/get-the-most-out/screenshots#review-screenshot-changes).
