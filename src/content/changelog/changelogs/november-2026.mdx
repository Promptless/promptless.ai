**Improvements:**

- **Review time estimates discount moved text:** Moved text, such as a split-out section, counts once at a lower rate than new writing. A reorganization shows the review time its edits need, so sorting by shortest review puts quick reviews first. See [Triage the review queue](/docs/for-docs/work-the-queue/assignment-and-routing#triage-the-review-queue).
