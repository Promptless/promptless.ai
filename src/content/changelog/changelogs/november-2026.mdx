**Improvements:**

- **Filter the Impact chart by doc collection:** On the Impact page (**Settings** → **Impact**), select one or more collections under **Collection** to chart only their suggestions. Select none to chart your whole organization. **Deleted doc collections** groups suggestions whose collection you removed from `promptless.yaml`, so the collections add up to the organization total. See [Measure the impact of Promptless](/docs/for-docs/measure/reporting-and-roi#find-the-signals-promptless-records).
