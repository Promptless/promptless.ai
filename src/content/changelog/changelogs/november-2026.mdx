**Improvements:**

- **Screenshots on every suggestion:** Screenshot thumbnails now appear on each suggestion's row and at the top of the suggestion, replacing the Screenshot Updates tab. Catch a wrong or stale screenshot while you triage, and filter with `has:screenshots` to review every visual change in one pass. See [Review screenshot changes](/docs/for-docs/get-the-most-out/screenshots#review-screenshot-changes).
- **Voice messages in Slack:** Promptless transcribes voice messages and audio files in threads you trigger it from and uses what was said in the suggestion. Talk through a change when that's faster than writing it up. See [Include images and files](/docs/for-docs/connect/triggers/slack-messages#include-images-and-files).
- **People settings page:** An admin can see and correct which Slack, GitHub, and GitLab accounts Promptless links to each teammate. Admins can also compare and merge duplicate records, and archive or delete stale ones. Teammates prove their own accounts by signing in, so the weekly digest tags the right assignees. See [Link teammates' accounts on the People page](/docs/for-docs/work-the-queue/assignment-and-routing#link-teammates-accounts-on-the-people-page).
- **Slack follow-ups by instruction:** Add an instruction to `PROMPTLESS.md` to have Promptless reply in a suggestion's Slack thread when the suggestion resolves. The instruction can cover merged and closed docs PRs and closed or archived suggestions, or only the cases your team cares about. It replaces the **Suggestion Status Updates** organization setting. See [Post status updates in Slack threads](/docs/for-docs/tune/notifications#post-status-updates-in-slack-threads).
