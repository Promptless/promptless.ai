**Improvements:**

* **Resume a pending Slack admin-approval install:** When your Slack workspace requires an admin to approve the Promptless app, Promptless keeps your pending request so you can leave and come back—the pending state persists across page reloads and sessions. This resumable handoff is now available from both onboarding and the Integrations settings page. Connecting opens Slack in a secondary window while the dashboard keeps a pending card that updates on its own once the install completes. The card gives you explicit actions: **Complete installation** to finish once an admin approves, and **Start over** to cancel the request. A connection attempt that doesn't finish keeps your request pending rather than dropping it. See [Slack integration](/docs/for-docs/reference/integrations/slack#when-admin-approval-is-required).
