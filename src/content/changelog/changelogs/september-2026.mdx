**Improvements:**

* **Promptless auto-joins your public passive-listening channels:** Promptless now automatically joins the public Slack channels you configure for passive listening, so they start receiving messages without anyone inviting it first. Private channels and channels shared with another workspace through Slack Connect still need you to add Promptless in Slack. See [Passive channel listening](/docs/for-docs/get-the-most-out/passive-channel-listening).
