**New Features:**

* **Propose Vale rules from your style guidance:** During doc collection analysis, Promptless now proposes Vale rules derived from your style guidance, records them in your Agent Knowledge Base and, when it can place them in your repository, opens a suggestion to bring them in, supplementing any Vale setup you already have. You review the proposed rules and decide whether an advisory rule becomes enforced. See [Propose Vale rules from your style guidance](/docs/for-docs/audit/standards-enforcement#propose-vale-rules-from-your-style-guidance).
