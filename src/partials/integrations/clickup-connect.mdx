You need a ClickUp account in the workspace whose task statuses you want Promptless to watch.

<Steps>
  1. On the Promptless [Integrations](https://app.gopromptless.ai/integrations) page, select **Connect ClickUp**.

  2. If ClickUp asks you to log in, log in with the ClickUp account you want Promptless to use.

  3. In ClickUp, authorize Promptless for the workspace.

  4. After ClickUp redirects you to Promptless, confirm that ClickUp shows as connected on the Integrations page.
</Steps>

Promptless creates a webhook in your ClickUp workspace that sends task status changes to Promptless. ClickUp calls a workspace a team, so the ClickUp card lists your authorized workspaces as teams.

Promptless watches one workspace. When the ClickUp account belongs to more than one, Promptless watches the first workspace ClickUp returns for it. To choose the workspace, connect with a ClickUp account that belongs to that workspace only.
