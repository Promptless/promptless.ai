You need an admin role in your Promptless organization to connect Linear. Promptless reads Linear with the permissions of the Linear account that signs in during the connection.

<Steps>
  1. On the Promptless [Integrations](https://app.gopromptless.ai/integrations) page, select **Connect Linear**.

  2. If Linear asks you to log in, log in to the Linear workspace you want to connect.

     <img src="/assets/linear-signin.png" alt="Log in to Linear screen with Google, email, SAML SSO, and passkey options" />

     <img src="/assets/linear-auth.png" alt="Google sign-in prompt for Linear with Cancel and Continue buttons" />

  3. After Linear redirects you to Promptless, confirm that Linear shows as connected on the Integrations page.
</Steps>
