To install Promptless in Microsoft Teams, you need:

- Microsoft Teams admin center access
- Permissions to manage app setup policies in your organization
- Ability to install and configure third-party apps

Connecting Teams has two stages. First, install the Promptless app into your Teams tenant (steps 1–3). Then link that tenant to your Promptless organization (step 4). The two stages are independent, and you can link before you install. Installing first, as numbered below, is the simplest path.

<Steps>
  1. Download the Promptless Teams app package from the [Promptless integrations page](https://app.gopromptless.ai/integrations).

     <img src="https://promptless-customer-doc-assets.s3.amazonaws.com/docs-images/org_2lvkgU9erOFxYhtEVVC0ymPrPdF/996a81d2-7bb6-4b6b-95d3-ef076c150450-teams-integrations-page-download.png" alt="Download Teams app package from Promptless integrations page" />

  2. Upload the Promptless Teams app:

     1. Go to the [Microsoft Teams admin center](https://admin.teams.microsoft.com).
     2. In the left navigation, under **Teams apps**, select **Manage apps**. Manage apps controls which apps users in your organization can install.
     3. Select **Actions** > **Upload new app**.
     4. Choose the Promptless Teams app package you downloaded.
     5. Select **Upload**.

     <img src="https://promptless-customer-doc-assets.s3.amazonaws.com/docs-images/org_2lvkgU9erOFxYhtEVVC0ymPrPdF/0042d96c-e3e7-42b7-8ee9-3d5cda74a0d4-teams-admin-center-app-setup-policies.png" alt="Manage Apps" />

     <img src="https://promptless-customer-doc-assets.s3.amazonaws.com/docs-images/org_2lvkgU9erOFxYhtEVVC0ymPrPdF/0042d96c-e3e7-42b7-8ee9-3d5cda74a0d4-teams-manage-apps-overview.png" alt="Teams Manage Apps Overview" />

     <img src="https://promptless-customer-doc-assets.s3.amazonaws.com/docs-images/org_2lvkgU9erOFxYhtEVVC0ymPrPdF/0042d96c-e3e7-42b7-8ee9-3d5cda74a0d4-teams-upload-custom-app-dialog.png" alt="Teams Upload Custom App Dialog" />

  3. Grant users access to the app:

     1. In the left navigation, select **Setup policies**.

        <img src="https://promptless-customer-doc-assets.s3.amazonaws.com/docs-images/org_2lvkgU9erOFxYhtEVVC0ymPrPdF/0042d96c-e3e7-42b7-8ee9-3d5cda74a0d4-teams-global-policy-configuration.png" alt="Teams Global Policy Configuration" />

     2. Select the **Global (Org-wide default)** policy.

     3. Turn on **Upload custom apps** and **User pinning** if they're off. Users need these settings to install and pin custom apps like Promptless.

     4. Under **Installed apps**, select **Add apps**.

        <img src="https://promptless-customer-doc-assets.s3.amazonaws.com/docs-images/org_2lvkgU9erOFxYhtEVVC0ymPrPdF/0042d96c-e3e7-42b7-8ee9-3d5cda74a0d4-teams-add-apps-button.png" alt="Global Policy" />

     5. Search for "Promptless", and add it to the policy.

     6. Select **Save**.

  4. Link your Teams tenant to Promptless. This stage happens in Promptless, outside the Microsoft Teams admin center:

     1. On the [Promptless integrations page](https://app.gopromptless.ai/integrations), find the **Microsoft Teams** integration.
     2. Select **Link App & Grant Read Channels**. This starts a Microsoft admin-consent sign-in that only a Microsoft admin can approve. If you're not an admin, ask your Microsoft admin to complete this step.
     3. Approve the sign-in. This links your tenant to your Promptless organization.
</Steps>

Until your tenant is linked, the Promptless bot can't map incoming Teams messages back to your organization. It replies that no workspace is connected.

If the button reads **Contact Promptless**, OAuth isn't configured for your organization yet. Email [help@gopromptless.ai](mailto\:help@gopromptless.ai) to finish connecting.

To let Promptless read files attached in Teams channels, a Microsoft tenant administrator also chooses which SharePoint sites it can read from. See [Reading files you share](/docs/for-docs/reference/integrations/microsoft-teams#reading-files-you-share).
