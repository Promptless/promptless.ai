One Atlassian Cloud connection covers both Jira and Confluence. You need an admin role in your Promptless organization to connect it. Promptless reads Jira and Confluence with the permissions of the Atlassian account that approves the connection. To connect a [dedicated Atlassian account](/docs/for-docs/reference/integrations/atlassian#provision-an-atlassian-account-for-promptless), log in to Atlassian as that account first.

<Steps>
  1. On the Promptless [Integrations](https://app.gopromptless.ai/integrations) page, select **Connect Jira Cloud**.

  2. If Atlassian shows its login screen, log in with the account you want Promptless to use.

     <img src="https://promptless-customer-doc-assets.s3.amazonaws.com/docs-images/org_2lvkgU9erOFxYhtEVVC0ymPrPdF/aab15aef-2759-42e6-93ec-d4c090526273-jira-atlassian-login.png" alt="Atlassian Login Screen" />

  3. On the OAuth consent screen, review the permissions Promptless requests. For what each scope is for, see [Permissions required](/docs/for-docs/reference/integrations/atlassian#permissions-required).

     <img src="https://promptless-customer-doc-assets.s3.amazonaws.com/docs-images/org_2lvkgU9erOFxYhtEVVC0ymPrPdF/aab15aef-2759-42e6-93ec-d4c090526273-jira-oauth-consent.png" alt="Jira OAuth Consent Screen" />

  4. Select **Accept**. If the consent screen shows no **Accept** option, your Atlassian account might not have the required permissions. Ask your Jira administrator to connect, or [invite them to Promptless](/docs/for-docs/reference/account-management).

  5. On the Promptless [Integrations](https://app.gopromptless.ai/integrations) page, confirm the connection on the Atlassian card.
</Steps>

To manage Promptless's access later, open Atlassian and go to your avatar > **Account settings** > **Connected apps**.
