Create a link that lets a person connect one integration to this organization in their browser. Send `connect_url` to someone who administers that tool. They sign in to Promptless and must be a member of this organization; the first person to open a link for an unclaimed organization claims it and becomes its owner. Call `get_account_status` after they finish to confirm the connection.

- **Annotation:** Changes data
- **Server paths:** `/mcp`, `/mcp/with-direct-edits`
