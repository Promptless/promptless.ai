Follow a Promptless task: wait up to 30 seconds for new messages, changed results, or completion. Omit cursor for an immediate initial snapshot; reuse the returned cursor to receive later messages. A timeout is normal: use `next_call` to continue waiting without a separate sleep. Drain `has_more_messages` even when finished is true. Read resolution and suggestions for the outcome. Use `get_task_status` for a one-time check. Waiting does not cancel cloud work.
